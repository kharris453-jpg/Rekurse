Grow a tunnel

Keep drive near 1.00–1.14×, persistence around 96–99%, then use a little positive zoom and spin. Raise one drive control at a time.

Find petals and wells

Pair mirror fold with 3–8-fold kaleidoscope. Add modest cross-coupling, then use detune for asymmetry. Cross-feed delay adds echoes but can muddy high coupling.

Make the seed stay visible

Use a fractal or image overlay around 20–45% with Screen, then Inject when you want the loop to digest it. Overlay preserves the source; injection transforms it.

Recover a flat field

With Homeostasis at 50% or more the loop pulls itself back within a few seconds; raise Energy target for a brighter field. Raw loop: for black, Pulse or move drive toward 1.00×; for white, lower gain or contrast—not both at once.

Abandon a path

Blank slate returns every dial to its as-loaded neutral and wipes the field completely — the clear button only erases pixels for a moment. Use it when a pattern has run its course and you want a genuinely fresh start.

Let the room play the plate

With the Chladni plate on, raise Mic drive: loudness excites the plate and the dominant pitch picks the figure — bass rings the big simple modes, highs the intricate ones. At 0 the plate follows the manual frequency knob; at 100% it follows the room.

Let the music play the plates

Raise Music plates and the spectrum plays three Chladni voices — bass rings big simple figures, highs the intricate ones, each tinted to its band. Figure morph rotates which mode family each band excites, so a bass hit can detonate an intricate figure. Material damping shapes all plate physics at once.

Keep color alive without glare

Chroma near 100% plus a slow hue velocity gives gradual migration. High chroma + fast hue + low display smoothing is the most likely harsh combination.

Before you start: flashing and moving light

Rekurse makes constantly changing patterns that can be bright, fast and flickering. Some people are sensitive to flashing or rapidly changing images (photosensitive epilepsy). If that is you or anyone nearby, or you feel unwell, dizzy or get a headache, stop (Pause loop, or close the page) and speak to a doctor.

The comfort limits (luminance ceiling, flash guard and the guardrails) are on by default. Turning them down, or turning the energy and music reactions up, makes stronger light, so go gradually.
